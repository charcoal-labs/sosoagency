A brand is more than a logo

Your logo is the first thing people see, but a brand is everything they experience after that: the colours on your delivery van, the feel of your business card, the tone of your website and the sign outside your door. When those pieces match, people trust you faster. When they don’t, even a great business can look smaller than it is.

As a branding agency in Johannesburg, we design complete corporate identities, not just a mark. We start with what makes your business different, turn it into a clear visual idea, and then carry that idea into every place your customers meet you.

What’s included in a Soso brand identity

  • Brand strategy and positioning – who you’re for, what you stand for and how you should sound.
  • Logo design – a primary logo plus alternate versions for small spaces, dark backgrounds and social media.
  • Visual identity – colour palette, typography, patterns, imagery style and graphic elements.
  • Brand guidelines – a clear guide so your team, printers and suppliers apply the brand correctly.
  • Corporate stationery – business cards, letterheads, email signatures, invoices and presentation templates.
  • Vehicle, signage and workwear branding – designs ready for your fleet, building and uniforms.
  • Packaging and merchandise – product packaging, gift items and branded apparel.

Branding for every kind of organisation

We’ve built identities for a water and irrigation company, an office furniture manufacturer, a florist, a mobile beauty clinic, a medical centre, a rural high school in Limpopo, a national labour union and community foundations. The industries are different, but the goal is the same: an identity that looks professional from day one and still works as the organisation grows.

Whether you’re a start-up that needs a logo and business cards, or an established company ready for a rebrand, we scale the work to what you actually need.

Rebranding an existing business

If your brand no longer reflects the quality of your work, a rebrand can change how customers, staff and investors see you. We review what you already have, keep the equity that’s worth keeping, and modernise the rest, so loyal customers still recognise you while new ones take you more seriously.

FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a branding project take?

A logo and core identity usually takes three to six weeks, depending on how many rounds of feedback are needed. Full rollouts with signage, vehicles and packaging take longer because they involve suppliers.

Do you only work with businesses in Johannesburg?

No. We’re based in Johannesburg and love meeting clients in person, but we work with organisations across South Africa remotely.

Will I own my logo and brand files?

Yes. When the project is complete you receive all final logo files and brand assets, and full ownership of the work.

Can you also build our website?

Yes. Many of our branding clients also get a website from us, so the brand and the site launch together and match perfectly.
