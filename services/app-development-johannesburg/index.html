From idea to app store

A good app starts with a clear problem to solve. Before anything is built, we work with you to understand who will use the app, what they need to get done and how the app supports your business. Then we design, prototype and test the experience so you can see and feel it before development begins.

We design and develop mobile apps in Johannesburg for start-ups and established businesses that want a product people actually keep on their phones.

What we do

  • Product discovery – defining the users, features and goals for a first version you can launch.
  • UI/UX design – user flows, wireframes and polished interface design that feels natural on each platform.
  • Interactive prototypes – clickable prototypes to test with users and show investors or stakeholders.
  • iOS and Android development – one codebase for both platforms wherever possible, to save time and cost.
  • App Store and Google Play launch – store listings, screenshots and submission.
  • Updates and support – improvements and new features after launch, based on how people use the app.

UI/UX design that people understand

The best apps feel obvious. We base our UI/UX design on research and testing, build consistent design systems, and add the small interactions that make an app feel fast and polished. If you already have an app that people find confusing, we can also redesign the experience.

Apps that match your brand

Because we also design brands and websites, your app will feel like part of the same family as the rest of your business, with the same look, tone and quality across every screen.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do you build apps for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. We design for both platforms and, where it suits the project, build with a single codebase so one app runs on iOS and Android.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on the features, integrations and platforms involved. After a short discovery conversation we give you a clear quote and can suggest a smaller first version to launch sooner.

Can you design an app that another team develops?

Yes. We can deliver UI/UX design and interactive prototypes ready for your in-house or partner developers.
